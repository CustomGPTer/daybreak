Privacy policy

Daybreak has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your alarms stay on your phone.

In short

The developer collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, in the app or on this website.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms, timers, settings and alarm history are kept on the phone. An automatic backup copy is saved in your phone's Download/Daybreak folder, and you can export a backup file whenever you like.

Who the app talks to

  • Public video sites, to find songs and save the one your next alarm needs.
  • radio-browser.info, a free public directory of radio stations.
  • Station pictures, from the addresses the directory lists, or from a station's own website when none of those work. Only the picture is fetched.
  • open-meteo.com, for the weather on the morning card and world clock. Only the place you chose is sent.
  • gov.uk, for the dates of UK bank holidays.
  • GitHub, once a day, to see whether a newer version is out. Only the version number is read.

Each of these sees your phone's internet address, as with any visit. None receives anything that identifies you.

Children

Daybreak is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
